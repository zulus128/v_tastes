Welcome to Tastes, a social discovery app built around real recommendations from people you trust.

The experience starts with a fast phone sign-in and one-time passcode. New members can create a profile, choose and crop a photo, set their name, username and location, then personalize the app with favourite dishes, places and appearance.

Permissions are requested in context, including location, contacts and notifications. The final onboarding step takes the member directly into the product.

The home feed brings together friends' reviews, photos, ratings and recommendations. Members can open a post, scroll through the full review, react, comment, share it, or save it into a personal folder for later.

Discover supports visual browsing, search and filters. Places can be explored as a list or on the map, with useful details such as category, rating, price and distance.

Activities turn recommendations into plans. A member can create an activity, invite friends, review the details and track responses after publishing.

Social profiles show posts, followers, favourites and saved collections. Settings include profile editing, preferences, appearance, notifications and account controls. Rewards make progress visible and encourage continued participation.

Messaging is connected to the same live test Firebase environment. Here, two independent phones are in the same conversation. A message sent from one person appears on the other phone immediately, and the reply arrives back in real time.

Together, these flows demonstrate the complete product loop: join, personalize, discover, save, share, plan and stay connected.
